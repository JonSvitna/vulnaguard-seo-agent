How hard should we park SEO + Content?

Email CRM is the face of the product. Pick how buried the old SEO/Content stuff gets.

A

Gone from nav

No sidebar links. Routes still work if you know the URL (/dashboard, /content-pipeline). Cleanest daily experience.

B

“Advanced / Tools” section

Collapsed footer group in the sidebar: SEO Agent, Content. Out of the way but one click if you need them.

C

Feature flag / env toggle

Completely hidden unless ENABLE_SEO_TOOLS=true. Default off in production. Strongest “this is an email CRM” signal.
